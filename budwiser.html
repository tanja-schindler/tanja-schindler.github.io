BudWiSer seminar series

The Budapest-Vienna seminar is a series of seminars that was originally running between the Dynamical Systems groups of the University of Vienna and BME Budapest. Subsequently, the groups at Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA) and the groups at Jagiellonian University and AGH in Krakow were added. The local organizers at Jagiellonian University are Adam Kanigowski, Dominik Kwietniak and Tanja Schindler. For the homepages of the other hubs BME Budapest, ISTA and University of Vienna
